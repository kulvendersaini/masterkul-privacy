Privacy Policy for Masterkul

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Masterkul ("we," "our," or "us") provides habit tracking and consistency management services through the Masterkul mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in using email/password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, display name, and unique authentication identifier (user_id).
  • Usage & Habit Data: We store your tracked streaks, completed days, timer activity, milestone badges, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Basic diagnostic logs and crash data to ensure application performance and stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage authentication and account sessions securely.
  • To synchronize your habit progress, streaks, and milestone achievements.
  • To display public ranking on the dynamic leaderboard (display name and streak metrics only).
  • To maintain application integrity and resolve technical issues.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols via Supabase. We enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring that your private journey records can only be read, modified, or accessed by your authenticated account.

4. Account and Data Deletion

We believe in full user autonomy. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly inside the app:

  1. Navigate to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm deletion.

Upon confirmation, your authentication profile, streaks, journey logs, milestone records, and leaderboard entries are immediately and irreversibly purged from our database via secure server-side procedures.

Alternatively, you may request account deletion by emailing us at: supportmasterkul@gmail.com.

5. Third-Party Services

Masterkul utilizes trusted third-party providers to facilitate core functionality:

  • Supabase: Backend database infrastructure and authentication services.
  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In: Authentication integration.

6. Children's Privacy

Masterkul is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
